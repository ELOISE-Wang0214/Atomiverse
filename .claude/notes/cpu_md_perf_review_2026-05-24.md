# CPU MD Performance Review — 2026-05-24

## Executive summary

CPU MD posture is **healthy but not yet competitive with a tuned LAMMPS at high core
counts on the MSU PE workload**. The hot path (LJ ket-loop with xsimd, OMP reduction
clauses on virials, member-state scratch in CellList) is well-engineered; the
thread-local force arena is the dominant correctness and scalability pattern. The
biggest remaining costs are concentrated in three places: (1) the
**per-thread O(n_force) zero+reduce** that every Evaluator pays (LJ, BondHarmonic,
AngleHarmonic, DihedralMultiHarmonic) — five separate reduce passes per step
where one fused pass would do, (2) the **multi-thread CellList rebuild** allocates
`emplace_back` into thread-local `std::vector<pair<int,int>>` and then does a
3-pass count/prefix/scatter that costs more than the work it does at PE-typical
densities, and (3) **MPI halo + bonded paths use `std::unordered_map<int64,int>` lookups
inside the per-tuple loop** — at 32–128 ranks with ~100 atoms/rank the hash-map
cost dominates the bonded force kernels. Bonded virials and KE reductions are
correct but several do their own `MPI_Allreduce` outside the existing
`ReduceEnergy` 18-double batch — two avoidable per-step Allreduces in `NoseHoover` /
`NoseHooverNPT`. Async pipeline shape is clean (drop_oldest, 3-slot ring, single
worker) and stays off the hot path.

## Good things to preserve

- **xsimd ket-loop in LJ** (src/cpu/potentials/pair_lj_cut.cpp:271-427) with hoisted-locals `lx/ly/lz/half_lx/...` is the right pattern; the inlined branchy min-image (documented ~7% gain over `box.min_image_batch`) is the right deviation. Don't refactor it back to use `Box::min_image_batch` member helpers.
- **Member-state scratch in CellList** (`thread_pairs_`, `thread_visited_`, `bin_write_ptr_` — src/cpu/md/cell_list.cpp:447-456, 166-174) is the "no allocation in launch()" contract correctly applied. Preserve when reworking the multi-thread CSR build.
- **Batched 18-double `ReduceEnergy::launch`** (src/cpu/md/mpi/reduce.cpp:43-67) is the right single-Allreduce idiom — every new per-rank scalar should be folded into this payload, never get its own Allreduce. Expand when adding new reduce-able fields.
- **NeighborList skin gating + size_stale short-circuit** (include/atomiverse/cpu/md/neighbor_list.h:107-137) cleanly mirrors LAMMPS `neighbor::decide()` and avoids redundant rebuilds; the dual gate (delay + every) is correct.
- **Async pipeline triple-buffered ring + drop_oldest** (include/atomiverse/cpu/analysis_executor.h, snapshot_publisher.h) keeps observer/recorder work off the hot path with a zero-subscriber fast path. The 5ns early-return makes thermo-on parity painless. Preserve the architecture; only the few `prealloc_slots` boundaries warrant audit.
